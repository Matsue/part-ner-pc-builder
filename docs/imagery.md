# Product imagery

The 30 catalog images in `public/products/` are individually generated fictional product concepts, not photographs of real products. They were made with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized to 768 × 768 and encoded as WebP for delivery.

The shared visual brief is a single, fully visible PC component in a dark charcoal studio, realistic materials, soft rim lighting, and no real brand logos or promotional text. Each product's category, color, and form factor informed its prompt. The listed product specifications remain authoritative; generated connector details are illustrative.

Exact prompt sets are retained in `docs/imagery/` alongside the image ID mappings. The storefront reuses each product's image consistently in the catalog, assistant recommendations, and selected build.

## USD demo pricing

Prices are fictional, denominated in US dollars, and exclude tax and shipping. The localization preserves relative prices and budget trade-offs, using a $2,000 example budget and a $1,970 representative white build. This is a demo repricing, not a live foreign-exchange conversion.
